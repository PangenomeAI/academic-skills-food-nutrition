**Publisher:** Oxford University Press (and partners).

## Publisher-wide guidance
**Oxford journals do not share one style.** These two differ in references, abstract and structure. Use the journal's block only.

## Food Quality and Safety
[Author guidelines](https://academic.oup.com/fqs/pages/author_guidelines)
- **References: name–year (author–date)** at submission.
- Abstract: research suggested ≤250 words, no citations; review unstructured ≤300; short communication ≤200.
- Short communication: ≤6 figures + tables. Perspective: ≤1,000 words, 1 display item, under 10 references.
- Graphical abstract encouraged (landscape).

## Nutrition Reviews
[Instructions](https://academic.oup.com/nutritionreviews/pages/general_instructions)
- Reviews and commentaries only; **no primary research**; unsolicited submissions welcome.
- Systematic reviews: **structured abstract** (Context, Objective, Data Sources, Data Extraction, Data Analysis, Conclusions); some other types unstructured.
- Keywords: the page gives both "at least three to five" and "5–7" — use 5.
- Reviews ≤50 double-spaced pages including references; commentaries under 2,000 words; line and page numbers.
- **References:** numbered superscripts, placed after commas and periods.
