# Niche Nutrition Journals — Author Guideline Skill

> **Verification status:** **Verified** for Obesity Facts, Current Opinion in Clinical Nutrition and Metabolic Care, Hepatobiliary Surgery and Nutrition and Food & Nutrition Research (2026-11-27). **Beneficial Microbes not verified** (site asks for a human check).
> Anything not listed here is unconfirmed: check the journal's guide, or ask the author to
> paste or upload it, before applying a limit.

Single journals from different publishers: **do not transfer rules between them.**

## Obesity Facts — now on Oxford Academic
[Author guidelines](https://academic.oup.com/ofa/pages/author-guidelines)
- Vancouver: numbered in order, **in parentheses**.
- Abstract ≤250 words, no citations or abbreviations. Research and review typically 5,000 words, 4–8 tables/figures; brief report 3,000.

## Current Opinion in Clinical Nutrition and Metabolic Care (Wolters Kluwer)
[Instructions](https://edmgr.ovid.com/cocn/accounts/ifauth.htm)
- **Commissioned reviews only.** About 2,500–3,000 words excluding references, double-spaced.
- Structured abstract ≤200 words (starts with "Purpose of review"); 3–5 keywords; Vancouver numbered.

## Hepatobiliary Surgery and Nutrition (AME)
[Guidelines](https://hbsn.amegroups.org/pages/view/guidelines-for-authors)
- Original article: no word limit; structured abstract (Background, Methods, Results, Conclusions) 200–450; 3–5 keywords; Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusions.
- Review ≤6,000 words, abstract 200–350. Vancouver references.

## Food & Nutrition Research
[Author guidelines](https://foodandnutritionresearch.net/index.php/fnr/author)
- Structured abstract **200–300** words (Background, Objective, Design, Results, Discussion, Conclusions); **5–10** keywords; popular summary ≤75 words.
- Vancouver, numbers in parentheses, DOIs; non-conforming papers are returned.

## Beneficial Microbes (Brill) — unconfirmed
Ask the author for the current guide.
