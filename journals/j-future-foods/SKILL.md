# KeAi / Tsinghua Food Journals — Shared Author Guideline Skill

> **Verification status:** **Verified** for Journal of Future Foods and The Crop Journal (KeAi guides, 2026-11-27). Food Science and Human Wellness and other titles are **not verified**.
> Anything not confirmed must be checked at the journal's guide before submission. Tell the
> author so, and ask them to paste or upload the current guide if it cannot be read.

## Publisher-wide guidance (compared across journals, 2026-11-27)
Shared by both: numbered references in square brackets; numbered sections with matching
cross-references; "Material and methods". **Abstract and keywords differ:**
Journal of Future Foods ≤300 words and ≤8 keywords; The Crop Journal 200–300 words and
3–5 keywords.

**Publisher:** KeAi (Elsevier-hosted) / Tsinghua University Press · Sources: [Journal of Future Foods GfA](https://www.keaipublishing.com/en/journals/journal-of-future-foods/guide-for-authors/), [Food Science and Human Wellness](https://www.sciopen.com/journal/2097-0765). Both are open access.

## Article types
Research articles; Reviews; Short communications; Perspectives.

## Manuscript structure
Title page → Abstract → Keywords → 1. Introduction → 2. Materials and methods → 3. Results and discussion → 4. Conclusions → declarations → References → figure/table legends. Continuous line and page numbering.

- **Journal of Future Foods (verified):** abstract ≤300 words; ≤8 keywords; **highlights and graphical abstract required**; sections numbered, with Results and Discussion listed separately; Word file; double-blind review.
- **Food Science and Human Wellness:** now hosted on [SciOpen](https://www.sciopen.com/journal/2097-0765) (Tsinghua University Press), not on a KeAi page; limits *unconfirmed*.

## Reference style
**Numbered (Vancouver / Elsevier numbered).** In-text `[1]`; numbered reference list with DOIs.

Example:
> [1] A.B. Author, C.D. Author, Title of the article, Journal of Future Foods 3 (2023) 123–130. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfutfo.2023.xx.xxx

## Figures & tables
Halftones ≥300 dpi, combination ≥500, line art ≥1000; vector preferred; ~90 mm / ~190 mm column widths; editable tables; cite in order.

## Submission checklist
- [ ] JFF: abstract ≤300 words, ≤8 keywords, highlights + graphical abstract · [ ] Numbered references with DOIs
- [ ] Declaration of competing interest · [ ] Data availability · [ ] CRediT contributions
- [ ] Line + page numbering · [ ] Figures ≥300 dpi · [ ] Cover letter
