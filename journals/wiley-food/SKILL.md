# Wiley Food Journals — Shared Author Guideline Skill

> **Verification status:** **Verified** on official guides (2026-11-27) for Comprehensive Reviews, Molecular Nutrition & Food Research, Food Frontiers and Journal of the Science of Food and Agriculture. Other titles are **not verified**; Wiley titles differ widely, so do not transfer rules between them.
> Anything not confirmed must be checked at the journal's guide before submission. Tell the
> author so, and ask them to paste or upload the current guide if it cannot be read.

## Publisher-wide guidance (compared across journals, 2026-11-27)
**There is no shared Wiley guide.** The four verified journals use Chicago author–date,
APA, Vancouver and a numbered no-title style, with abstract limits from 200 to 260 words.
Use only the journal's own block below; for an unverified Wiley journal, ask for its guide.

**Publisher:** Wiley · Confirm each journal's Author Guidelines on Wiley Online Library (each journal's "Author Guidelines" tab). Structure below reflects the common Wiley template.

## Verified journal rules (2026-11-27)
These override the common template below.

### Comprehensive Reviews in Food Science and Food Safety
[Guide](https://www.ift.org/trends-and-learning/research-and-publications/scientific-journals/comprehensive-reviews-in-food-science-and-food-safety/crfsfs-author-guidelines/)
- Main body **8,000–15,000 words** (excl. refs, tables, figures); structured review method (e.g. PRISMA).
- Abstract preferably ≤250 words, one paragraph, no headings.
- Title page → Abstract → Introduction → Body → Conclusions → declarations → References → tables → figures.
- **Chicago author–date**; alphabetical list. Numbered references are returned.
- Cover letter explaining novelty against reviews of the past 3 years; double-spaced, continuous line numbers.
- Figures ≥300 dpi at twice published size; graphical abstract not required.

### Molecular Nutrition & Food Research
[Guide](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/16134133/homepage/2216_forauthors.html)
- Research ≤6,500 words and review ≤8,500 **including references, legends, tables**; short communication ≤5,000 and ≤5 display items.
- Abstract ≤200 words; ≤5 keywords, alphabetical; double spacing.
- Numbered sections: 1 Introduction → **2 Experimental Section** → 3 Results → 4 Discussion → References. If 3 and 4 are combined, follow with "Concluding remarks".
- References: superscript numbers in square brackets after punctuation; all authors (no *et al.*), **no article title**; abbreviated journal in italics, **year bold**, volume italic, first page.
- Isolated dietary compounds: add a dosage information paragraph. Graphical abstract: see guide §5.16.

### Food Frontiers
[Guide](https://iadns.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/26438429/author-guidelines)
- Original article about ≤5,000 words, review about ≤8,000 (incl. abstract; excl. refs, tables, figures); short communication ≤4,000, ≤30 refs.
- Abstract ≤260 words; 3–6 keywords; **APA** references.
- Original articles: Introduction → Methods → Results → Discussion → Acknowledgment → Disclosure → References.
- Graphical abstract published (50 × 60 mm) with a 2–3 sentence summary.

### Journal of the Science of Food and Agriculture
[Guide](https://scijournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/10970010/homepage/forauthors)
- Research and review ≤6,000 words, short communication and mini-review ≤4,000 (**including** tables and figures, excluding references).
- Abstract ≤250 words; research and short communications **structured: Background, Results, Conclusion**; 4–6 keywords.
- **Vancouver** numbered references.
- Figures: photos ≥300 dpi, graphs and drawings ≥800 dpi.

## Common Wiley structure
Title page → Abstract → Keywords → Introduction → Materials and Methods → Results → Discussion (or Results and Discussion) → Conclusion → Acknowledgements → Conflict of Interest → Author Contributions → Data Availability Statement → References → Tables → Figure legends → Figures.

- **Abstract:** unstructured, typically ≤250 words (review journals may allow more).
- **Keywords:** typically 5–6.
- **Novelty/impact statement:** some journals request a short novelty statement.

## Reference style
Most Wiley food journals use **author–date**; several are APA 7th-style. In-text `(Author & Author, 2023)`; alphabetical list.

Example (APA-style):
> Author, A. B., & Author, C. D. (2023). Title of the article. *Food Science & Nutrition*, 11(4), 1234–1245. https://doi.org/10.1002/fsn3.xxxxx

For `eFood` (numbered): in-text `[1]`, numbered list.

**Food Science & Nutrition** (official excerpt): abstract 250 words, 4–6 keywords. Clinical titles **Obesity** and **JPGN** are numbered (Citation Style Language repository).

## Figures & tables
Figures ≥300 dpi (line art ≥600–1000 dpi); TIFF/EPS/PDF; RGB online; editable tables; cite in order. Wiley provides an EarlyView production workflow.

## Ethics & open access
Most listed titles are Gold open access (APC). Include ethics/consent statements for human/animal studies and an ORCID for the corresponding author.

## Submission checklist
- [ ] Correct reference style (author–date for most; numbered for eFood)
- [ ] Abstract ≤~250 words · [ ] 5–6 keywords · [ ] Novelty statement (if required)
- [ ] Conflict of interest + Author Contributions + Data Availability statements
- [ ] ORCID for corresponding author · [ ] Figures ≥300 dpi · [ ] Cover letter
