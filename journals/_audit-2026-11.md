# Journal guideline audit — 2026-11-27

Scope: all 25 publisher-tiered journal skills. Result: **verification is incomplete**,
because many publishers block automated access. Verification status is stated in each
skill. Evidence: **O** official page read in a browser; **S** search-engine excerpt of an
official page; **C** Citation Style Language repository (secondary, often years old).

## Still unverified — check at the journal before submitting

- Entirely or almost entirely: Wiley, Taylor & Francis, Science family, Emerald (BFJ),
  Oxford (FQS, Nutrition Reviews), Annual Reviews, Quality Assurance and Safety of Crops
  & Foods, Agriculture & Food Security limits, niche nutrition and society agriculture
  journals, most multidisciplinary titles.
- Individual journals inside partly verified skills (most Elsevier titles, most Springer
  and Nature-family titles, MDPI titles other than Foods). The ~230 agriculture and other
  coverage-map journals were **not** checked one by one.
- A coverage-map row routed to a publisher skill does not mean that journal shares its
  reference style.

Open conflict: the Citation Style Language repository lists `Horticulture Research`,
poultry and animal science titles with their own styles; they were not changed.

To close a gap: open the journal's guide, paste or upload it, and update the skill.
