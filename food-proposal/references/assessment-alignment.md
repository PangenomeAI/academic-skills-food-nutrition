# Assessment-Criteria Alignment

A funded proposal answers the scheme's **assessment criteria** point by point. Write to
them, and check coverage before review.

## Map content → criteria
For each scheme, keep an explicit map: **criterion → where the proposal answers it**
(heading + paragraph). `compliance_gate` flags any criterion with no answer.

- **ARC EOI / Full:** address the stage's Assessment Criteria (Grant Guidelines; Full
  Application notes the **weightings** — spend words in proportion). Answer every
  numbered question under `PROJECT QUALITY AND INNOVATION` (and, for Full, `BENEFIT` and
  `COMMUNICATION OF RESULTS`). Write for a **general assessor**: define acronyms, explain
  significance to a non-specialist, use sub-headings/bolding and boxed key
  questions/benefits.
- **UoM Ag & Food project (/100):** Presentation & structure /15 (layout, numbered
  titled tables/figures, consistent accurate referencing, **AI-use in
  Acknowledgements**), Introduction & Aims /30 (background, need & relevance, **clear
  research gaps**, scope-appropriate justification, clear achievable aims/objectives),
  Literature Review /30 (**analysed at depth** for the scope), Methods /10 (justified;
  linked to objectives), Risks /5 (**likelihood/impact matrix compulsory**, back-ups,
  compliance), Resources /5 (consumables, facilities, costs, time inputs), Timelines /5
  (**GANTT** or similar).

## Rules
- Every criterion visibly addressed — an unaddressed criterion loses marks/ranking even
  if the science is strong.
- Grounded: claims of significance, novelty, and benefit are argued from the evidence
  base (Stage 1), not asserted. Keep calibrated hedging; no over-claiming.
- Where a scheme mandates an element (risk matrix, GANTT, First Nations research
  strategy, AI-use statement), it **must** be present.
