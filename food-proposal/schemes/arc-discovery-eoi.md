# ARC Discovery Projects — Expression of Interest (EOI)

**Funder:** Australian Research Council · **Document:** Project Description (EOI stage)
**Verified:** 2026-08 · **Source:** ARC Discovery Projects EOI Instructions to Applicants.
Always confirm the current year's Instructions and Grant Guidelines before submitting.

## Hard limits
- **Length: no more than 2 A4 pages.** (Word-count proxy ≈ 1,300 words; confirm in the
  real template — figures/tables and font affect page fit.) Check with
  `python3 scripts/proposal_wordcount.py <draft>.md --scheme arc-discovery-eoi`.
- Font per ARC Instructions §2.4; **references may be 10-point**.

## Required headings — use these, in this order
1. **PROJECT TITLE** — may differ from the form's A1 title; may exceed 10 words.
2. **PROJECT QUALITY AND INNOVATION** — answer, with sub-headings:
   1. How does the research address a significant gap in knowledge or problem?
   2. What are the key research questions?
   3. What methods and/or conceptual/theoretical framework will be used?
   4. What is the anticipated new knowledge the project will create?
   5. How might the research deliver economic, environmental, social and/or cultural
      benefits to Australia?
3. **REFERENCES** — all references, including the participants' relevant prior work;
   identify pre-prints per §2.5; may be 10-pt.
4. **ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS (if required)** — significant idea/authorship contributions by
   people not named in the application. Delete the heading if not needed.

## Audience & assessment
- Assessed by **General Assessors** (College-of-Experts members), **not** detailed
  discipline experts — **avoid unexplained acronyms and field jargon**; write for an
  intelligent non-specialist.
- Address the **EOI-stage Assessment Criteria** explicitly.
- **Formatting is your friend:** sub-headings, bolded key phrases, and boxed key
  research questions/benefits make the description easy to assess.
