# ARC Discovery Projects — Full Application

**Funder:** Australian Research Council · **Document:** Project Description (Full
Application) · **Verified:** 2026-08 · **Source:** ARC Discovery Projects Full
Application Instructions to Applicants. Confirm the current Instructions and Grant
Guidelines (incl. subsection 6.4 assessment criteria and weightings) before submitting.

## Hard limits
- **Length: no more than 7 A4 pages**, in the required format. **All text — including
  text within figures and tables — must follow §2.4 formatting.** (Word-count proxy
  ≈ 4,550 words; confirm in the real template.) Check with
  `python3 scripts/proposal_wordcount.py <draft>.md --scheme arc-discovery-full`.
- References may be 10-point.

## Required headings — use these, in this order
1. **PROJECT TITLE** — may differ from A1; may exceed 10 words.
2. **PROJECT QUALITY AND INNOVATION**
   1. How does the aim contribute to an important gap in knowledge, or address a
      significant problem?
   2. How is the research innovative in the context of recent international advances?
   3. What are the major research questions?
   4. Describe the project design, including conceptual framework and methods.
   5. Does the project involve research pertaining to Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait
      Islander communities? If yes, describe collaboration, engagement, relationship
      and capacity building, and benefit sharing.
   6. To what extent will the research enhance national and/or international
      collaboration?
3. **BENEFIT**
   1. What new or advanced knowledge will result?
   2. What are the potential economic, commercial, environmental, social and/or
      cultural benefits for Australia and/or international communities?
4. **COMMUNICATION OF RESULTS** — how will the results be communicated?
5. **REFERENCES** — all references incl. participants' prior work; pre-prints per §2.5;
   may be 10-pt.
6. **ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS (if required)** — as for the EOI; delete if not needed.

## Assessment
Address the **Full-Application assessment criteria** (Grant Guidelines §6.4) and note
their **weightings**. Formatting aids assessment (sub-headings, bolding, boxed
questions/benefits). Write for assessors who may not share your specialty.
