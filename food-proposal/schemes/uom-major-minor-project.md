# UoM Ag & Food — Major/Minor Research Project Proposal

**Funder/venue:** The University of Melbourne (coursework research project) ·
**Verified:** 2026-08 · **Source:** Ag & Food Project Proposal Marking Guideline v6
(2025). Confirm current subject requirements with your coordinator.

## Hard limits & format
- **Total 1500 ± 150 words** — but the count covers **only**: **Abstract,
  Introduction, Aims & Objectives, Literature Review, and in-text citations**.
  **Not counted:** cover sheet, table of contents, tables/figures, methods,
  acknowledgements, reference list, risks, resources, timeline, appendix.
  Check with `python3 scripts/proposal_wordcount.py <draft>.md --scheme uom-major-minor-project`.
- **11-point font, 1.5 line spacing.**

## Prescribed structure (in order)
1. **Cover Sheet** (not counted)
2. **Abstract** *(counted)*
3. **Table of Contents** (not counted)
4. **Body of Proposal** — **Introduction**, **Aims & Objectives**, **Literature
   Review** *(all counted)*; 1.5 spacing, 11 pt.
5. **Methods** (not counted)
6. **Acknowledgements** (not counted) — **must state AI use**: which model(s) and what
   prompts were used (if applicable).
7. **Reference list** (not counted)
8. **Risks, Resources, Timelines, Figures/Diagrams/Tables** (not counted).

## Marking rubric (/100) — write to earn these marks
- **Presentation / structure — /15:** laid out as prescribed; tables/figures well used,
  numbered, titled; correct spelling/grammar; consistent, accurate, complete
  referencing; **Acknowledgement states AI use (model + prompts)**.
- **Introduction, Aims & Objectives — /30:** clear background; the need for the
  research and its relevance (industry/scientific) explained; **research gaps clear**;
  justification fits major/minor scope; aims and specific objectives clear, relevant,
  achievable in the timeframe.
- **Literature Review — /30:** relevant literature reviewed and **analysed at
  appropriate depth** for the project scope.
- **Methods — /10:** methods explained and justified; clear link between objectives
  and methods.
- **Risks — /5:** project-specific and general PM risks identified and analysed with a
  **likelihood/impact matrix (compulsory)**; back-up planning; University compliance
  (risk assessment, induction, ethics) identified.
- **Resources — /5:** consumables; faculty resources/facilities; cost estimates; time
  inputs (hours) for key tasks.
- **Timelines — /5:** tasks scheduled; **GANTT chart or similar** included.
